Our Story

Two Decades of
Trusted Workmanship

After setting up as a self-employed sole trader over 20 years ago, MPC Plastering & General Building has built a strong reputation for quality workmanship, reliability and professionalism — primarily through word-of-mouth alone.

Over the years, we've established a loyal and repeat client base, providing services for fellow trade contractors, landlords, commercial clients and homeowners alike. Thanks to the continued support of our customers and recommendations from satisfied clients, our business has continued to grow year after year.

As a result, MPC has expanded into a larger workforce, allowing us to take on a wider range of projects while maintaining the high standards and personal service we've always been known for.
