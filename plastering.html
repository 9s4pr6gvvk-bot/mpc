All Aspects of Plastering

We cover the full range of plastering and rendering — from traditional craftsmanship to modern finishes.

Skimming & Re-skimming

A smooth, seamless skim coat applied over existing plaster or plasterboard — bringing tired walls and ceilings back to a perfect finish ready for decoration.

Get a quote

Rendering

Sand and cement render, monocouche and through-colour render systems applied to external walls — durable, weatherproof and sharp in appearance.

Get a quote

Pebble Dashing

Traditional pebble dash applied to a high standard — a popular and long-lasting external finish for Yorkshire homes that stands up to the weather.

Get a quote

Heritage & Lime Mortars

Lime plaster and mortar work for older properties — breathable and sympathetic to the original structure of the building.

Get a quote

Coving

Neat, cleanly finished coving fitted to walls and ceilings — a simple touch that makes a real difference to the look of any room.

Get a quote

Patch Repairs

No job too small — damp patches, cracks, holes and damaged sections repaired and blended seamlessly into the existing surface.
